Our Story

Section 1 — Origin

Every Great Journey Begins with a Single Paddle Stroke.

Established in 1999, A.M. Rieta's story is a journey of faith, perseverance, and purpose. Our founder and President, Mr. Alvin M. Rieta, started with a paddle that represents our humble beginnings.

"I am grateful for all the pulling and stretching I've experienced. Despite the pain, paddling helped me find and hone my purpose."
— Mr. Alvin M. Rieta, Founder & President
"Every great journey begins with a single paddle stroke."

From a paddle yesterday to machines and equipment today, A.M. Rieta has truly grown and continues to grow. It may not be a perfect company, but it is the right one.

Our Compass

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

As we celebrate over 25 years of perseverance and grace, we look forward to a future where A.M. Rieta Corporation continues to support brands through responsible manufacturing, ethical sourcing, and quality-driven formulations.

We strive to inspire trust, nurture lasting partnerships, and create products that make a meaningful difference in people’s lives.

Our Mission

Customer Focus: To support brands with flexible and customized manufacturing solutions tailored to their unique requirements.

Compliance & Safety: To uphold ISO 22716 GMP, FDA, and international regulations for product safety at every stage of production.

Strong Partnerships: To build lasting relationships with clients, suppliers, and stakeholders founded on trust and transparency.

Employee Growth: To create a positive work environment that fosters continuous learning, development, and well-being.

Our Values
