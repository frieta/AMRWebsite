Quality System

Our Quality Policy & Premises

Quality Policy

We are committed to producing and delivering safe, consistent, and reliable products at reasonable cost, while meeting customer expectations and applicable regulatory requirements.

Quality control is applied from raw material receipt up to finished product release.

We follow a quality management system aligned with GMP standards.

All testing is performed using regularly calibrated equipment.

• Full documentation and traceability are maintained for every batch.

• We continuously review and improve our quality systems and processes.

Quality-Controlled Production Facility

Our facility is designed with clear and controlled movement of personnel, materials, and equipment to help prevent cross-contamination and product mix-ups at every stage of production.

Laboratory Facilities

Laboratory Facilities

Our laboratory is designed and maintained in accordance with Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) requirements to support careful and reliable testing processes, helping ensure that every product meets our quality standards before release.

Microbiology

Micro Lab Facility

An isolated room crucial for infection prevention and control. This dedicated facility is used for comprehensive microbial analysis to ensure every product is safe from biological contamination before release.

Products undergo a thorough 3–5 day testing cycle prior to release, covering all required microbiological parameters.

Microbial Analysis (BAM methodology)
Environmental Swab Testing
Gram Staining procedures
Annually calibrated Incubators
Annually calibrated Autoclaves
Biosafety Cabinets (BSC)
Physicochemical

Physicochemical & Organoleptic Laboratory

A well-ventilated, safe workspace where trained analysts perform comprehensive testing across a full range of physical, chemical, and sensory product attributes.

All instruments are calibrated annually to guarantee accurate and reproducible testing results across every production batch.

Appearance & Color Analysis (Organoleptic)
pH Testing & Measurement
Viscosity Determination
Electrical Conductivity Testing
Stability & Compatibility Testing
Annually calibrated instruments throughout
